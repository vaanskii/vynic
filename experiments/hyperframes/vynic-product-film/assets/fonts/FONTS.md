# Fonts in the film

All fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (text in `Geist-OFL.txt`;
the same licence covers Noto and Cormorant Garamond with their own copyright
lines). No proprietary font is embedded.

| Font | Files | Used for | Why this font |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Geist, Geist Mono | `Geist-*.ttf`, `GeistMono-Variable-latin.woff2` | Vynic brand, POS UI, captions, data | Vynic's brand sans (platform-web) |
| Noto Serif / Noto Sans | `NotoSerif-*.ttf`, `NotoSans-*.ttf` | SaaS "classic" website skin | restaurant-web's classic theme stack is `"Noto Serif Georgian", … Georgia` / `"Noto Sans Georgian"`; the Noto Latin companions are the open equivalent of that stack for English text |
| Cormorant Garamond | `CormorantGaramond-*-latin.woff2` | CUSTOM (bespoke) website skin: display + menu | an OFL high-contrast display serif for the dark, gold editorial register |
| Noto Serif Italic | `NotoSerif-Italic.ttf` | CUSTOM skin gold italic accent word | the bespoke reference site sets its accent words in Georgia italic (proprietary); Noto Serif Italic is the open substitute |

Sources: Noto from the LibreOffice font bundle on this machine; Cormorant
Garamond from the HyperFrames `embedded-captions` skill's bundled fonts; Geist
as before. The bespoke reference site's own header font is not used (licence
unknown, and it is that client's identity).
